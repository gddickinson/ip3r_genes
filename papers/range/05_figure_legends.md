## Figure legends

**{fig:separation}.** Two profile models separate the IP₃ receptors from the
ryanodine receptors over the whole census, and the band in which neither is
called is drawn rather than described. Each record's score against the IP₃
receptor profile is plotted against its score against the ryanodine receptor
profile, and the relative margin between them is shown as a distribution with
the same band marked. A call requires the winner to clear 30 bits over 200
match states and to beat the loser by more than 10 % of its own score. The
margin is relative because bit scores grow with alignable length, so a fixed
gap would call every full-length protein and refuse every fragment. Few
records fall in the band beside the two dense clouds, which is why the
merged call can be treated as settled.

**{fig:range}.** The family is present across the animals, several protist
lineages, the green algae and the early-diverging fungi, and absent from land
plants, Dikarya and every prokaryote. Each bar gives the fraction of a
lineage's swept reference proteomes that carry at least one IP₃ receptor
call, with the count over the denominator printed beside it, and lineages are
grouped under the sweep group they belong to. Counting proteomes rather than
records keeps a heavily sequenced phylum from outvoting a sparse one. Bars at
zero sit in kingdoms where the same search finds the family elsewhere and
finds the promiscuous MIR domain in the empty lineages themselves.

**{fig:chase}.** Every plant and fungal record ends as a real gene or a
fragment, and none sits near the identity that marks contamination. Each
record's verdict from seven ordered rules is shown with its best identity to
any protein outside its own kingdom. An assembly contaminant would be 95–100 %
identical to one particular animal, while a deep homologue sits at 20–40 %;
every record here falls in the second range, so the green-algal and
early-fungal records are genes of those organisms, and the land-plant and
Dikarya absences are losses rather than the family's edge.

**{fig:absence}.** All 35 clade-level absences hold in genome assemblies that
carry a working positive control. For each clade the number of controlled
genomes is drawn beside the number searched, and only controlled genomes
count on either side. A clade whose control bar were short would be standing
on nothing, which is why the two counts are drawn together. No clade has a
full-length receptor in any controlled assembly; two land-plant assemblies
carry only scattered tblastn traces.

**{fig:copies}.** Copy number outside the vertebrates runs from zero to
eighteen, and the vertebrate paralogue count of three is drawn as a reference
line rather than a category. Each point is one genome's count of complete
receptor gene models, grouped by taxonomic group. A flatworm carries eighteen
and a ciliate thirteen, so expansion in this family is not specific to the
vertebrates.

**{fig:fold}.** The fold separates the two families as the sequence census
does, on a scale calibrated on this panel before any structure was called.
(**a**) Each structure's best TM-score against the IP₃ receptor references is
plotted against its best against the ryanodine receptor references, both
normalised by the reference. (**b**) Every class of structure pair is drawn
against TM-align's published random (0.17) and same-fold (0.5) bars: negative
controls stay below the same-fold bar, and different conformations of one
paralogue score higher than different paralogues. Recovering the family split
from coordinates alone matters because the two families share every
diagnostic domain.
