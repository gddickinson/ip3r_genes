## Figure legends

**{fig:modules}.** The pore module is more conserved than the ligand core
until the luminal loop is counted as pore, and then all three paralogues
reverse. (**a**) Dark blocks mark the binding module at the N-terminus of
each paralogue, coloured blocks the channel at the C-terminus, red the
excluded stretch and grey the other Pfam domains. (**b**) Each box summarises the
core-minus-pore identity difference, one value per orthologue, with the loop
excluded (left, the primary definition) and included (right, as InterPro
draws the domain); the dashed line marks zero. Both definitions share one
axis because the sign of the answer turns on that single boundary.

**{fig:shells}.** Constraint declines smoothly across the ligand pocket and
shows no step at the contact radius. (**a**) Each point is one pocket
residue, placed at its median all-atom distance to IP₃ over six structures
and coloured by paralogue; open circles are the consensus contacts, grey
lines mark the shell boundaries, and the dashed line is the whole-protein
mean. (**b**) Bars give the mean conservation per shell and paralogue. A
contact-driven model predicts a drop beyond 4.5 Å, and none appears, which
is why the constrained unit is read as the pocket.

**{fig:omega}.** Per-site substitution rates reproduce the pocket but not the
module ranking. (**a**) Bars compare the mean nonsynonymous rate of the
ligand core, the pore module and the whole protein in each paralogue.
(**b**) Lines trace the share of sites FEL calls purifying in each shell,
which falls from every contact site to a floor in the outer shells. The rate
axis uses a different alignment and statistic from the identity axis, so
agreement on the pocket is corroboration and disagreement on the modules is
reported rather than resolved.

**{fig:lineage}.** Receptors in lineages without the enzyme that makes IP₃
show no relaxation of the ligand core once divergence is matched. (**a**)
Bars give the share of receptor-carrying proteomes in each sweep group that
lack a phosphoinositide-specific phospholipase C, with counts beside each
bar; no vertebrate proteome lacks one. (**b**) Each point plots one non-vertebrate sequence's paired difference
against its divergence from human IP₃R3; red records sit among distant
sequences, which is the confound matching removes, and violet diamonds
(ryanodine receptors) show where a ligand-free core falls at that distance.
(**c**) The curve gives the matched test's power against the true shift,
with the observed shift and the ryanodine receptor control marked, so the
null is read against what the design could detect.
