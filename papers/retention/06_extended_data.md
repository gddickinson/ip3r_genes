## Extended Data

**{fig:status}.** Each of the 927 genome × paralogue cells carries one ledger
status, stacked per paralogue from the strongest evidence to the weakest.
Four cells reach the absent status, and all four are in the two cyclostome
genomes. They remain the strongest negative the search itself produces, and
the state rules show that they are paralogue labels the panel cannot resolve.

**{fig:confound}.** Recovery rises with assembly contiguity, and the
contiguity bar splits the scope into two populations. (a) The fraction of
cells found is plotted in bins of contig N50 that are placed on the bar
rather than across it. (b) The same recovery is collapsed to the 189 genomes
above the bar and the 120 below it. A sliding window across the bar would
report a recovery rate that no genome in the window has.

**{fig:synteny_reach}.** Synteny cannot place the fragments because their
contigs carry nothing to read. (a) Each rescue region is placed by the
number of coding genes and informative flanking symbols on its own contig,
with the caller's four-key floor drawn. (b) The caller's call rate and
accuracy are plotted on the same key axis. The caller is right whenever it
calls, so what fails here is reach.

**{fig:reconstruction_bar}.** Moving the reconstruction bar across its own
measured uncertainty changes no cell. (a) Each of the 43 reassembled cells is
placed against the bar and its gap, on the row of the rule that would catch
it if the bar rose; the within-row offset is a point's rank, so the drawing
is reproducible. (b) The count of absent cells stays at zero with both
protective rules on until the bar is raised far beyond the gap.

**{fig:mk_profile}.** The rate models are refused because the character never
changes, and the refusal is measured. (a) The likelihood is profiled along a
rate grid for every model, axis and branch-length scheme, and every curve runs
to the edge of the grid. (b) Every rate that can be fitted in the sensitivity
grid rises with the number of cells its setting made absent, so it describes
the filter rather than the family.

**{fig:integrity}.** Lesion density follows bait identity far more than
assembly contiguity. (a) Frameshifts and stops per thousand aligned residues
are plotted against identity to the bait. (b) The same density is plotted
against contig N50. (c) The paired within-genome test compares each cell
with its own genome's other family loci, which removes the assembly. Density
is logarithmic with a zero band, because most intact loci carry no lesion.

**{fig:lesion_strata}.** The one surviving lesion signal is a bird result
that the contiguity control leaves unresolved. (a) The identity-matched sign
test is run within each vertebrate class. (b) The bird *ITPR3* pairs are split
at the contiguity bar. (c) The loci above the lesion bar that any fossil
reading flags are counted, and all are full-length models. Most of the bird
evidence lies below the bar, where an indel excess cannot be told apart from
an assembly effect.
