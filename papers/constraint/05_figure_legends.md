## Figure legends

**{fig:omega}.** All three paralogues sit more than twenty-fold below
neutrality. Each bar is a paralogue's one-ratio ω from the full codon set,
the bar beside it repeats the estimate with every genome gene model removed,
and the dashed line marks ω = 1. The axis is logarithmic because the result
is the distance below neutrality, which a linear axis would flatten onto the
floor. Removing the reconstructed gene models barely moves any bar, which shows
that they do not drive the rates.

**{fig:branches}.** Two statistics on two models separate ITPR1 from its
sisters in the same direction. Panel (a) sets each paralogue clade's
foreground ω against the background ω of the rest of the family in a
two-ratio model. Panel (b) gives the RELAX selection-intensity parameter k
for the same three contrasts, where k above 1 means intensified and below 1
relaxed. A point-estimate contrast and a test on whole rate distributions
agree that ITPR1 is held more tightly than ITPR2 and ITPR3.

**{fig:elements}.** The gate and the selectivity filter are the most
conserved elements of the receptor when composition is left out of the
metric, and the luminal loop is the least conserved on both metrics. Panel (a)
ranks every element of the three paralogues by mean Jensen–Shannon
divergence on the deep layer; panel (b) repeats the ranking on the
modal-residue fraction, which uses no background frequencies. Dashed lines
give each protein's linker mean, the within-protein control. The two metrics
agree on the extremes, so neither is a product of scoring transmembrane
residues against a background table.

**{fig:profile}.** Conservation collapses in the luminal loop and recovers
on both sides of it in all three paralogues. Each row plots the
modal-residue fraction along the pore-forming half of one paralogue in its
own numbering, in ten-residue bins that never cross an element boundary, so
the edge of the loop is drawn where the structure puts it. The loop, the
filter and the gate are coloured, and the dashed line is that protein's
linker mean. The loop was located from the membrane geometry of 6DQN, so the
dip is a prediction the profile confirms rather than a boundary drawn on it.

**{fig:sites}.** The measured functional residues clear the easy control
and most of them clear the hard one. Panel (a) scores the ten IP₃ contacts,
the two filter-lining and the two gate-lining residues against the whole
protein and against the rest of the element each sits in. Panel (b) gives
identity between each pair of human paralogues for every element, with the
gate at full identity in all three pairs and the luminal loop lowest. Beating
the rest of its own element is what shows that the structural annotation
picks out residues its domain does not already share.

**{fig:classifier}.** The family-wide layer classifies clinical variants
best, and the deep within-paralogue layer ranks third. Panel (a) draws
receiver-operating curves for the four layers on the fixed set of pathogenic
and benign positions that every layer scores, as full curves because the
layers cross. Panel (b) places the variants of uncertain significance on the
same axis against their gene's labelled medians. A resource for this family
should quote the layer at the top of panel (a), and panel (b) is a
stratification, not a call.
