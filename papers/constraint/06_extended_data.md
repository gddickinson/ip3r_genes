## Extended Data

**{fig:codon_checks}.** Two checks on the codon models show why every rate
here comes from a tree and from the best of several optimiser runs. Panel (a)
plots dN against dS for every pair of tips within each paralogue on log–log
axes, with the neutral diagonal and the saturation bar at dS = 1.5; most
pairs lie past the bar in all three paralogues. Panel (b) plots every
branch-site restart against its own nested null likelihood, and the three
points below the line are local optima that a single-start run would have
reported as answers.

**{fig:codon_alignment}.** The trimmed codon alignment carries every rate in
this paper. Panel (a) plots per-codon occupancy across the 57 tips, marking
the columns trimming kept; panel (b) gives each tip's coverage grouped by the
selection set the tree placed it in; and panel (c) counts the codons that
validation masked, by the route each coding sequence came from. The axis is
in codons, since a nucleotide axis would show a threefold difference that is
not in the data.

**{fig:paralog_alignments}.** The deep alignments behind the constraint map
thin out in places, and the figure shows where. Panel (a) counts how many of
the 249 to 265 orthologues cover each residue of the human reference; panel
(b) shows the shape screen, with a ring on the single chimeric model it
removed; panel (c) sets the four conservation layers beside the depth
control. Scores are less reliable where coverage thins than
where it is full.

**{fig:constraint_on_channel}.** Painting the constraint map onto one
experimental structure of each paralogue shows the most conserved residues
on the pore and leaves the luminal loop grey. Panels (a) to (c) colour each
trace from the B-factor column the painting step wrote, and panel (d) paints
the per-site selection layer onto ITPR3. Unscored residues appear in grey
rather than at the low end of the scale, which keeps missing data from
reading as an absence of constraint in the loop, where the two coincide.

**{fig:variants_on_structure}.** Pathogenic positions concentrate in the
elements that gate and select. Panels (a) and (b) place every labelled
variant on the ITPR2 and ITPR3 structures allowed to carry a human position;
panel (c) records the numbering rule for each candidate structure, which the
two ITPR1 structures fail; and panel (d) tests the enrichment of pathogenic
positions per element, with a ring where the test survives correction.
An infinite odds ratio is drawn at the ceiling with a marker so that the
strongest enrichment stays on the axes.
